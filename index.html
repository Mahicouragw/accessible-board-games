Made for every player

  • Audio-first: every move announces itself in clear speech and crafted sound — no screen required to play.
  • Sound: recorded cricket crowd effects are used under CC BY 4.0; other cues use browser synthesis. See the audio credits.
  • Multiplayer: local pass-and-play on one device, or online rooms synced in real time via Supabase.
  • Spectators & chat: watch live, send messages, and inspect any player's profile before and during a match.
  • Accessible: high contrast, large targets, full keyboard control, live region announcements, and reduced-motion support.
